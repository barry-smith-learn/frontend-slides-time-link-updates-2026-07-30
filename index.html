Product Overview

Problem Statement

Current exports are duplicative and brittle

  • Platforms: Camber, CentralReach, TREDS, Service Logs, more...
  • Each platform needs its own payroll export.
  • Changing our billing platform requires building a new export from scratch.
  • Payroll rules are complex. Building each export is costly.
  • Admins edit timesheets during manual, pre-payroll audits. Original record is obscured. 🤯
Development Duo

Two teams are shaping the solution

LEARN will standardize source data, while Upstart13 builds the rules engine and user experience.

Punch In/Out Time

Weighing the pros and cons

PROS
  • Fulfills legal time keeping requirements
  • UKG calculates overtime and meal premiums
  • Improves visibility for employees
  • Enables supervisor review/approval
CONS
  • Excessive UKG processing time
  • Unclear exception reporting
  • Manual payroll adjustments
  • Approval workflow labor cost

Automated enforcement

  • Process termination requests
  • Record payroll adjustments
Payroll Adjustments

Automation window

On Payroll Mondays, adjustments are automated for dates in the current month or last two months.

Example:Pay period Jan 31 - Feb 13. Payroll pulls Monday, Feb 15. Adjustments for work dates in Dec, Jan, Feb are automated.
