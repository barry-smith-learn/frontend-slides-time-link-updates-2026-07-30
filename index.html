Problem Statement

Current exports are duplicative and brittle

  • Platforms: Camber, CentralReach, TREDS, Service Logs, more...
  • Each platform needs its own payroll export.
  • Changing our billing platform requires building a new export from scratch.
  • Payroll rules are complex. Building each export is costly.
  • Admins edit timesheets during manual, pre-payroll audits. Original record is obscured. 🤯
Development Duo
